# Business Story Insights

## Description

Analyze Indian retail mart sales results and turn findings into business recommendations. The documented insights examine city-level revenue differences and the concentration of revenue among a small customer base.

## Learning outcomes

- Interpret sales and customer metrics in a business context.
- Identify geographic performance gaps and customer concentration risks.
- Formulate recommendations based on observed data.
