# Day 19 - Data Warehouse

This day extends the retail sales analytics workflow with Power BI performance analysis, business storytelling, and DAX-based reporting. The exercises include Linux command practice, review of report rendering metrics, interpretation of sales insights, and building calculated columns, measures, and slicers from Indian retail mart data.

## Tasks

1. [10 Linux Commands](<Task 1 - 10 linux commands/description.md>) - Practice common Linux commands and capture terminal output.
2. [Power BI Performance Analyzer](<Task 2 -  Performance Analyzer/description.md>) - Record visual timings and identify potential report bottlenecks.
3. [Business Story Insights](<Task 3 -  Business Story Insights/description.md>) - Interpret retail sales patterns and develop data-driven recommendations.
4. [DAX Functions](<Task 4 -  Dax Functions/description.md>) - Define DAX measures for core retail business KPIs.
5. [New Columns and New Measures](<Task 5 -  New Columns & New Measures/description.md>) - Load retail data into Snowflake and Power BI, then add calculated columns, measures, and slicers.
