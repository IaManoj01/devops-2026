# Day 19 - Data Warehouse

This day extends the retail sales analytics workflow with Power BI performance analysis, business storytelling, and DAX-based reporting. The exercises include Linux command practice, review of report rendering metrics, interpretation of sales insights, and building calculated columns, measures, and slicers from Indian retail mart data.
