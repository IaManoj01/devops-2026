# Blue-Green Deployment Strategy

## Description

Explore a Blue-Green deployment using separate blue and green EC2 environments. The evidence records environment creation, access to the green environment, a change made in that environment, and screenshots of the blue and green environments before and after a swap.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the Blue-Green approach of maintaining two parallel application environments.
- Compare the recorded blue and green environment states before and after a swap.
- Understand how validating a candidate environment before directing users to it can support safer releases.
- Recognize the role of rollback planning when retaining a previous environment.
