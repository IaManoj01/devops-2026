# NumPy, pandas, and Matplotlib E-commerce Analysis

## Description

Analyze an e-commerce orders dataset with pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib. The notebook inspects and cleans order records, derives revenue and month fields, summarizes revenue by month, region, and product, and creates charts to visualize the results.

## Learning outcomes

- Inspect dataset structure, summary statistics, and duplicate records.
- Clean text categories, parse dates, convert numeric fields, and handle invalid or missing values.
- Calculate order revenue and aggregate sales by month, region, and product.
- Visualize regional revenue, order-revenue distribution, monthly trends, and the relationship between quantity and revenue.
