# Use Cases of NumPy and pandas in Companies

Review examples of how companies use Python's NumPy and pandas libraries. The material connects data-library capabilities with practical business and analytics needs.

## Learning outcomes

- Recognize common uses of NumPy and pandas in organizations.
- Connect data-processing libraries with practical business problems.
- Identify where Python data tools can support analysis workflows.
